Location permission is requested only when you start GPS.

Walk the perimeter and record corners in order. Accuracy is shown in metres. Phone GPS is for approximate boundaries; use tapes or survey equipment for planting positions.

02 Planting rules

Crop spacings are editable starting assumptions, not site-specific recommendations.

Bearing 0° runs north–south; 90° runs east–west. Setbacks also apply around exclusions and paths. Rows are individually clipped to irregular edges.

Leave room to work.

Draw around a pond, building or unplantable patch. Add a straight access path at a chosen width.

03 Material & yield assumptions

Material assumes one plant per position. The extra allowance affects procurement only. Yield is a user-defined estimate; the layout does not assess soil, slope, drainage or crop competition.

04 Take the plan to the field

Pegging sheets give east/north offsets from the reference corner, row bearing, first and last plants, count and spacing. Establish a reference line on site before pegging.

Your boundary and plans stay in your browser. Online map tiles, when enabled, request the visible location from OpenStreetMap. Boundary geometry is not a legal land survey.
