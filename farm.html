02 · EXPECTED YIELD

Assumptions you can inspect.

Starting numbers are examples, not crop-specific yield forecasts. Enter figures for your variety, growing conditions and stated period. Weather and pest observations do not automatically alter these assumptions.

03 · WATER & IRRIGATION

Can the source meet the need?

Effective rainfall and efficiency are editable assumptions, not measurements. Daily net demand = max(0, ET₀ × Kc − effective rain); gross demand accounts for efficiency. This simplified balance does not model soil storage, wetted fraction, salinity, head loss or irrigation zoning. Confirm Kc and source yield locally. FAO irrigation guidance

04 · LABOUR PLAN

05 · WEATHER AT YOUR FIELD

The next seven days.

Loading weather sends only latitude and longitude to Open-Meteo. Forecasts are model estimates for the grid near your field, not on-site measurements or a guarantee of rain.

Weather data: Open-Meteo · Provider terms. Check soil moisture before irrigation; weather alone does not establish a disease or a safe spray window.

06 · CROP HEALTH

Look out for these diseases and pests.

Know the early signs, check the likely cause, and choose management for your cropping system.

Before using a pesticide

Ingredient names are reference options, not confirmed local approvals. Confirm the crop and cause, current country registration and exact product label. Follow label rates, PPE, harvest and re-entry intervals, pollinator precautions and resistance guidance. Organic eligibility depends on the formulation and certifier. No dose or spray schedule is supplied here.
