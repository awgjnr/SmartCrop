03 · WATER & IRRIGATION

Can the source meet the need?

Effective rainfall and efficiency are editable assumptions, not measurements. Daily net demand = max(0, ET₀ × Kc − effective rain); gross demand accounts for efficiency. This simplified balance does not model soil storage, wetted fraction, salinity, head loss or irrigation zoning. Confirm Kc and source yield locally. FAO irrigation guidance

Before using a pesticide

Ingredient names are reference options, not confirmed local approvals. Confirm the crop and cause, current country registration and exact product label. Follow label rates, PPE, harvest and re-entry intervals, pollinator precautions and resistance guidance. Organic eligibility depends on the formulation and certifier. No dose or spray schedule is supplied here.
